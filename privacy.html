Overview

Overview

Saathi ("we", "our", "us") is a read-only voice and visual digital companion built for older adults and first-time smartphone users in India. Saathi helps users navigate everyday smartphone tasks step by step with calm spoken guidance and clear on-screen highlights.

Saathi is engineered from the ground up with strict on-device privacy and zero automated tapping. This Privacy Policy explains what permissions Saathi requests on Android, how data is protected on your device before anything leaves your phone, and how you stay in full control of your information.

Section 1 · Prominent Disclosure

1. Android AccessibilityService API (Read-Only)

Saathi uses the Android AccessibilityService API solely during an active, user-initiated help session to read on-screen button labels, layout hierarchy, and occasional downscaled screenshots of allowed apps. This allows Saathi to place a visual highlight around the next button and speak short instructions aloud so the user can tap it themselves.

  • Strictly Read-Only — Zero Automated Actions: Saathi never taps, types, swipes, or performs gestures on the user's behalf. Gesture and action permissions are omitted from the app configuration, and read-only enforcement is audited at build time.
  • Automatic Banking & Payment Step-Out: When strict banking or payment apps open (such as UPI, net-banking, or payment checkout screens), Saathi immediately removes its overlay and automatically disables itself using disableSelf().
  • Protected Sensitive Screens & Private Messages: Saathi never reads UPI, PIN, or password screens, SMS messages, Gmail, or WhatsApp message contents. Even when the user explicitly consents to step-by-step button guidance inside WhatsApp, chat names and message contents remain hidden and screenshots are never captured.
Summary for Google Play Disclosure: The AccessibilityService API is used exclusively to provide read-only visual and spoken navigation assistance during active user-requested sessions. It is never used for remote control, automated clicking, call recording, or harvesting personal messages or financial credentials.
Section 2

2. On-Device Data Masking & AI Processing

Before any request leaves your phone, Saathi runs an on-device privacy filter that automatically detects and masks sensitive personal details—including phone numbers, financial amounts, email addresses, and UPI IDs (as well as spoken digit sequences).

When cloud assistance is needed to guide you through a screen, only your masked speech/text queries and masked UI labels (and occasional downscaled screenshots of allowed non-sensitive apps) are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to Saathi's Google Cloud backend powered by Vertex AI / Gemini.

Our AI backend operates with data storage explicitly disabled (store: false). Your queries and screen context are processed transiently to return the next spoken instruction and are never logged, retained, used to train AI models, or sold to any third party.

Section 3

3. Microphone & Speech Recognition

Saathi requests microphone access solely so you can speak your questions naturally in Hindi (Hinglish), English, or Kannada.

By default, Saathi uses Android's on-device speech recognition so your voice stays on your phone. Voice audio is sent to Google's speech recognition service only if you explicitly opt into online speech recognition inside Advanced Settings. Saathi does not record audio in the background when you are not actively talking to it.

Section 4

4. Optional Sound Finder (NotificationListenerService)

Notification Access is completely optional and disabled by default. It is only requested if you choose to turn on the Sound Finder helper under Advanced Settings → Sound Finder.

When enabled, this permission is used exclusively on-device via Android's MediaSessionManager.getActiveSessions() to identify and tell you which app is currently playing audio (for example, to help locate unexpected video or music playback). Zero notification titles, messages, sender names, or alerts are ever read, stored, or transmitted.

Section 5

5. On-Device Storage, Daily Quotas & Voluntary Reports

  • Local-Only Lists & Reminders: Your shopping lists, to-do lists, and reminders are stored strictly locally on your device and are never synced to Saathi's servers.
  • Anonymous Daily Quotas: To protect the service from abuse and enforce fair daily usage limits, Saathi stores an anonymous SHA-256 hash of the device ID in Google Cloud Firestore solely for rate-limiting and daily quota enforcement. This hash cannot be reversed to reveal your identity, phone number, or Google account.
  • Voluntary Error Reports: Saathi stores masked turn context (via /v1/report) only if you explicitly tap "Galat hai / Report" to flag an incorrect or confusing instruction so we can fix bugs and improve guidance quality.
Section 6

6. Data Deletion & Contact Us

You are always in complete control of your data:

  • Instant Local Deletion: Uninstalling Saathi or clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Saathi → Storage → Clear data) immediately and permanently deletes all local shopping lists, to-do lists, reminders, preferences, and local logs on your device.
  • Cloud Quota & Report Deletion Requests: If you have previously submitted a voluntary /v1/report entry or would like us to delete any associated quota records stored in Firestore, please email us with the approximate date/time of the report or request and we will delete the matching records promptly.
